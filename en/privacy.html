2. Overview

Bibelstunde is built to be deliberately data-minimal. We only process the data technically required to complete a purchase and provide your bar access. There is no user tracking, no advertising cookies and no sharing of data for marketing purposes.

3. Hosting

This website and the app are hosted by Vercel Inc. (340 S Lemon Ave #4133, Walnut, CA 91789, USA). When you access the pages, Vercel processes technically necessary server log data (including IP address, time of access, requested URL, browser type) to ensure delivery and security of the service. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, functional operation). A data processing agreement is in place with Vercel; the transfer to the USA is safeguarded by standard contractual clauses.

4. Purchase & payment processing (Stripe)

For payments we use the payment service provider Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (The One Building, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland). During checkout you provide your email address and your desired bar name; payment details (e.g. card data) are collected and processed exclusively by Stripe — we do not receive or store full payment data. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Details on Stripe's data processing: stripe.com/privacy.

5. Access credentials & storage

After a successful purchase we create a bar access. For this we store:

  • the bar name you chose (your access code),
  • your email address (to send your credentials and for support),
  • a hashed password (we do not store the plain-text password),
  • the Stripe purchase reference to associate the payment.

This data is stored in a database (Upstash Redis, operated via the Vercel Marketplace integration). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. The data is stored for as long as your bar access exists and is deleted on request (see "Your rights").

6. Login (JWT)

When you log in to the app, a technically necessary session token (JSON Web Token) is issued and stored locally in your browser to keep you logged in. This is storage strictly necessary for the functionality (§ 25 (2) TDDDG); no tracking takes place.

7. Email delivery (Brevo)

To send your credentials and transactional emails we use Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, 106 Boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France). Your email address is transmitted to Brevo for this purpose. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Brevo's privacy notice: brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy.

8. Fonts (Google Fonts)

This website embeds the "Montserrat" typeface via Google Fonts. When a page loads, a connection is made to Google servers (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), transmitting your IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a consistent presentation). Note: to avoid this transfer, the font can alternatively be self-hosted. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Cookies & tracking

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Only the technically necessary login-token storage described above is used. A cookie banner is therefore not required.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise your rights, simply email flogge@rockthehill.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy whenever our data processing changes or legal requirements make it necessary. The version published on this page at any given time applies.
